Data source: ESPN's public stats pages (NFL) and CollegeFootballData.com (CFB) — not hand-typed. College football is limited to Power 4 conferences (ACC, Big 12, Big Ten, SEC) plus Notre Dame, the set Yahoo Fantasy College Football uses. Two categories can't be sourced from any free public data and are flagged rather than guessed: Sacks Allowed (OL) isn't tracked publicly anywhere, and CFB Forced Fumbles isn't tracked by any free CFB data source we could find (NFL Forced Fumbles is available). A small number of CFB defensive linemen are tagged generically "DL" with no DT/Edge split, so they're excluded from both of those position boards rather than guessed into one.
